---
title: After a card is lost or stolen: replacement and reimbursement
---
This page explains what happens once the opposition has been placed.

## Police report
If your card was stolen or used by someone else, report it to the police and keep a copy of the report. It may be requested when your file is reviewed. A police report is not needed for a card that was simply lost and never used.

## Replacement card
A new card is ordered automatically after an opposition and normally arrives by post within 7 working days. It has a new number and a new PIN, sent separately. Remember to update any online shops and subscriptions that stored the old card details.

## Payments made before the opposition
Check your recent transactions and flag any you do not recognise as soon as possible. Unauthorised payments are reviewed case by case. A limited amount may remain at your charge for payments made before the opposition, unless the law provides otherwise. Payments made after the opposition are not charged to you.

## If you find the card again
Do not use it. A card under opposition is permanently cancelled. Cut it through the chip and dispose of it.
