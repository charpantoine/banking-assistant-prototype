---
title: Blocking your card: temporary lock and opposition
---
There are two ways to stop a card from being used. The right one depends on whether you expect to get the card back.

## What to do first if your card is lost or stolen
Place an opposition straight away, at any hour of the day. Open the mobile app, go to Cards, choose the card and select "Report lost or stolen". If you cannot reach the app, call the card emergency line shown on the bank's website, or go to a branch during opening hours. Note the time of your request and keep the reference you receive.

## Temporary lock
A temporary lock suspends payments and withdrawals while you look for a card you have probably misplaced. It takes effect immediately and can be reversed at any time from the mobile app. Direct debits and standing orders on the account continue to run.

## Opposition
An opposition is permanent. Once it is placed the card can never be used again, even if you find it later, and a new card must be issued. Use it whenever the card has been lost for good, stolen, or its details have been used without your consent.

## Which one should I choose?
Choose the temporary lock if the card is probably at home or in another bag. Choose an opposition if you know the card is gone or if you see a payment you do not recognise. If you are unsure and a day has passed, place an opposition.
