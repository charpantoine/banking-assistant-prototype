---
title: International payments and using your card abroad
---
This page covers payments outside the euro area and payments in other currencies.

## Paying by card abroad
Your card works in most countries. Payments in another currency carry a fee of 2% of the amount, and withdrawals in another currency carry a fee of 2% plus EUR 3. The exchange rate is the card network's rate on the processing day.

## Sending money abroad
For a transfer outside the euro area, you will need the recipient's account number, the BIC of their bank and the reason for the payment. These transfers cost EUR 20 and take two to five working days. Intermediary banks may deduct their own charges.

## Receiving money from abroad
Give the sender your IBAN and BIC. Incoming payments in euros are free. Incoming payments in other currencies are converted to euros and cost EUR 10.

## Before you travel
Check your card's payment limit in the app and raise it temporarily if necessary. Keep the card emergency line, shown in the app, in case your card is lost during your trip.
