---
title: Closing an account
---
You can close an account at any time and at no cost.

## How to request closure
Send a signed request by secure message in the app or hand it in at your branch. Say which account you want to close and give the IBAN of the account that should receive the remaining balance.

## Before closing
Cancel or move your direct debits and standing orders, and make sure the balance is not negative. Keep enough money to cover payments that are still pending, such as recent card purchases.

## How long it takes
Closure normally takes up to 30 days, which allows pending payments to clear. You will receive a confirmation once it is complete.

## Cards and documents
All cards linked to the account stop working on closure. Download your statements beforehand, because access to the app ends for that account. Statements can still be requested from an advisor afterwards.

## Joint accounts
A joint account can only be closed with the signature of every account holder.
