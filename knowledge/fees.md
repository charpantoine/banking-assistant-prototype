---
title: Account and card fees
---
The amounts on this page apply to everyday personal accounts. Fees are charged to the account they relate to.

## Account maintenance
Keeping an everyday account open costs EUR 2 per month. Savings accounts have no maintenance fee.

## Cards
A standard debit card costs EUR 40 per year and a premium debit card costs EUR 100 per year. The annual fee is charged in the month the card was first issued. Replacing a card after an opposition costs EUR 10.

## Cash withdrawals
Withdrawals in euros at any cash machine are free for the first 4 each month, then EUR 1 per withdrawal. Withdrawals in other currencies are described on the international payments page.

## Incidents
A rejected payment for insufficient funds costs EUR 15. An overdraft that exceeds the agreed limit costs EUR 8 per incident.

## Changes to fees
Any change to these fees is announced at least two months before it applies, by message in the mobile app.
