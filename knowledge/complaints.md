---
title: Making a complaint
---
If something has gone wrong, you can ask for it to be reviewed. The process has several steps, each going further if you are not satisfied.

## First step: your advisor
Contact your advisor by secure message, by phone or at your branch. Explain what happened and attach any useful documents. Most requests are settled at this stage.

## Second step: the complaints department
If the answer does not satisfy you, write to the complaints department through the Help section of the app. You receive an acknowledgement within 5 working days and a reply within 30 days.

## Third step: independent mediator
If the disagreement remains after the complaints department has replied, you can refer the matter to an independent banking mediator free of charge. The mediator's contact details are given in the department's final reply.

## What to include
State your customer number, the dates and amounts concerned, what went wrong and what outcome you are asking for. Never include your password or full card number.
