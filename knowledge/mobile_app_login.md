---
title: Logging in to the mobile app
---
The mobile app is protected by a personal password and by your registered phone.

## First login
Download the app from your phone's official store. Enter your customer number and the temporary code you received by post, then choose a six-digit password. Your phone then becomes your registered device.

## Forgotten password
On the login screen, select "Forgotten password". After confirming your identity with a code sent by text message and a photo of your ID document, you can set a new password.

## Account blocked after failed attempts
After five wrong passwords, access is blocked for your protection. Use the "Forgotten password" option to unblock it, or contact an advisor if that does not work.

## New phone
To move to a new phone, install the app on it and log in. Confirm the change from your old phone. If you no longer have it, contact an advisor to register the new device.

## Biometric login
Once you are logged in, you can enable fingerprint or face recognition in Settings. Your password remains valid as a fallback.
