---
title: Contacting an advisor
---
Several channels let you reach a person at the bank, depending on how urgent your request is.

## Secure messaging
Send a message from the mobile app under Help. An advisor normally replies within two working days, and the exchange stays in your message history.

## By phone
The customer service number is shown in the Help section of the app. Advisors answer from Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00, and on Saturday, 09:00 to 17:00. You will be asked to identify yourself before any account is discussed.

## In person or by video
Book an appointment with your advisor from the app. Video appointments are available on the same days as phone support.

## Urgent situations
For a lost or stolen card, do not wait for an advisor: place an opposition in the app or through the card emergency line, which is available at all hours.
