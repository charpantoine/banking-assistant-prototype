---
title: Overdraft
---
An overdraft lets your account balance go below zero up to an agreed limit for a short time.

## Agreed overdraft
An agreed overdraft is a limit set with the bank, based on your income. You can request or change it with an advisor. The limit appears in the account details in the app.

## Interest
Interest is charged only on the days the balance is negative and only on the amount overdrawn. The rate for an agreed overdraft is 8% per year, and it is shown on your monthly statement.

## Going beyond the limit
If a payment would take you beyond your limit, it may be rejected or accepted at the bank's discretion. Either case can result in the incident fee described on the fees page.

## If you are having difficulty
If your account stays overdrawn, contact an advisor early. Together you can review a repayment schedule or adjust your limit.
