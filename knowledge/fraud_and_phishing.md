---
title: Fraud and phishing
---
Fraudsters often pretend to be a trusted organisation to obtain your details or make you approve a payment.

## How to recognise phishing
Be suspicious of any email, text message or call that creates urgency, asks you to click a link, or asks for codes or passwords. Look for unusual sender addresses and pages that imitate a familiar site.

## What the bank will never ask
The bank will never ask for your password, your full card number, your PIN or a confirmation code, whether by email, text message or phone. It will never ask you to move money to a "safe account".

## If you received a suspicious message
Do not click on anything and do not reply. Delete the message after forwarding it to the fraud team through the Help section of the app.

## If you shared details or approved a payment
Act quickly: place an opposition on your card, change your app password and contact an advisor. Report the facts to the police and keep a copy of your report.

## Suspicious activity on your account
Check your transactions regularly. If you see one you do not recognise, flag it in the app so that it can be investigated.
