---
title: Understanding and finding your IBAN
---
An IBAN is the international number that identifies a bank account. You give it to anyone who needs to pay you or to set up a direct debit.

## Where to find your IBAN
In the mobile app, open the account and select "Account details". You can display it, copy it or download a statement of account details as a PDF. It is also printed on your account statements.

## What an IBAN looks like
It starts with a two-letter country code and two check digits, followed by the identifiers of the bank and the account. French IBANs have 27 characters; the length varies by country.

## Is it safe to share my IBAN?
Sharing your IBAN so that someone can pay you carries little risk. Still, only give it to people and companies you trust, and check your statements for direct debits you did not authorise. Never share your card details, passwords or confirmation codes along with it.

## BIC
The BIC identifies the bank itself. It appears next to the IBAN in the account details and is sometimes requested for payments outside Europe.
