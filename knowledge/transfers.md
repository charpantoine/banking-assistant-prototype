---
title: Making a bank transfer
---
Transfers can be made from the mobile app, from the website or at a branch.

## Adding a beneficiary
Before a first transfer to someone, add their name and account number as a beneficiary in the app. For security, a new beneficiary becomes usable after a confirmation step on your phone and a waiting period of up to 24 hours.

## How long a transfer takes
A standard euro transfer reaches the other account within one working day when ordered before 16:00. An instant transfer arrives within about ten seconds, every day of the year, and is limited to EUR 5,000 per transfer.

## Limits
The standard limit is EUR 10,000 per day from the app. To raise it for a one-off payment, contact an advisor.

## Standing orders
A standing order repeats a transfer every week, month or quarter. You can create, change or stop it in the app up to the day before the next payment.

## Cancelling a transfer
A transfer with a future date can be cancelled up to the day before. An executed transfer cannot be cancelled. If you sent money to the wrong person, contact an advisor quickly so a recall can be requested; the recipient's agreement may be needed.
