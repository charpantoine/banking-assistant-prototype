---
title: Branch opening hours policy
---
Each branch sets its own timetable within common rules. This page describes those rules, not the timetable of a particular branch.

## General rules
Branches generally open from Tuesday to Saturday, and many close for lunch. Timetables differ according to location and staffing.

## Public holidays
Branches are closed on public holidays. On the day before some holidays, a branch may close earlier than usual; this is announced at the branch door and in the mobile app.

## Appointments
Some subjects, such as opening an account or discussing a large transfer, are handled by appointment. You can book one in the app, and it can be held in person or by video.

## Outside opening hours
Most everyday operations remain available in the mobile app at any time. Cash machines located in the branch lobby stay accessible outside opening hours in most locations.
